Purchases are made through the store you use (the Apple App Store or Google Play) and follow that store's terms, payment and refund rules. Packs and bags give randomised items. Their odds are shown in the game before you buy and on the odds page; in some countries random packs cannot be bought at all. Purchases never decide a match alone: skill and tactics do. To restore purchases, see the support page.
